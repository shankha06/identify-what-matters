Most important addition: "issue accounts"

This gives you an issue-level sentiment map, which is much more useful than a simple "BJP sentiment = +X%" metric.
Suggested prototype architecture
We would actually reduce your initial universe to approximately 100–150 accounts, rather than trying to scrape everything.
Tier 1 — Core political accounts
~50 accounts
Party + major leaders.
Tier 2 — State/regional political accounts
~25–40 accounts
UP, Delhi, Maharashtra, Bihar, Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana, Punjab, Kerala, Rajasthan, etc.
Tier 3 — Media/commentary
~25–30 accounts
Mainstream + independent + political commentators.
Tier 4 — Youth/meme/activist
~15–25 accounts
Student organizations + political meme/viral accounts.
Tier 5 — Government baseline
~10–15 accounts
PMO, PIB, MyGov, ministries, ECI, state governments.
That gives you roughly:
125–160 Instagram accounts
which is a very reasonable prototype dataset.
One important correction to your current requirement – Do not hard-code labels such as:
"Pro-BJP"
"Pro-Congress"
"Anti-BJP"
"Anti-Congress"
as ground truth.
Instead use:
ACCOUNT
   ↓
POST
   ↓
Political / Non-political
   ↓
Issue / Topic Detection
   ↓
Entity Detection
   ↓
Sentiment
   ↓
Target / Direction
   ↓
Narrative Classification
   ↓
Engagement
   ↓
Undercurrent Score
For example:
Post: "Unemployment has reached alarming levels..."
could be:
Sentiment: Negative
Issue: Employment
Target: Government
Narrative: Governance criticism
Political direction: Anti-incumbent
Engagement: 2.4M
But another post saying:
"Government creates 10 lakh new jobs..."
could be:
Sentiment: Positive
Issue: Employment
Target: Government
Narrative: Government achievement
Political direction: Pro-incumbent
This is much more analytically defensible.
For 3-day prototype
Define the dataset as:
~150 Instagram accounts × 3 days × posts/reels/comments/engagement metadata
and build the dashboard around:
Volume → Engagement → Sentiment → Issue → Political Target → Narrative → Emerging Undercurrent
That will make the prototype look like a genuine Political Intelligence / Social Listening system, rather than just an Instagram sentiment scraper.
